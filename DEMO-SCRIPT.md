# StudyMate demo script

For the GenHacks demo video. The video must be **up to 3 minutes**, uploaded to YouTube or Vimeo with embedding turned on. This script runs about 2 minutes 30 seconds at a normal speaking pace. A silent 23-second walkthrough of the app is in `video/studymate-walkthrough.webm` if you want to record your voice over it, but recording your own screen while you talk will look better.

## Before you record

- Run the app: `npm run dev`, then open the link it prints.
- Clear the browser data for the site first, so the sample deck is fresh: in Chrome, open DevTools (F12), Application tab, Local storage, delete `studymate.state`, then refresh. The Biology basics deck and the two sample subjects will come back.
- Record in a quiet place. A phone propped up facing the laptop screen works, or use any free screen recorder.
- Speak slowly and clearly. It is fine to pause between sections and join the clips.

## Script

**0:00 - Intro (show the Flashcards tab with the deck list)**

"Hi, my name is Ibrahim, and this is StudyMate, a study helper I built for GenHacks. A lot of students revise with loose notes and a phone timer, and it is hard to know what you have actually covered. StudyMate puts flashcards, a quiz, a study timer and weekly targets in one place. It works in the browser, and everything is saved on your own device."

**0:30 - Flashcards (click Study on Biology basics, flip a card, mark one "Know it", add a card)**

"First, flashcards. StudyMate comes with a Biology basics deck so you can start right away. I click a card to see the answer. If I know it, I mark 'Know it', and if not, 'Still learning', so I can see what needs more work. I can shuffle the deck, and I can add my own cards, like this one. Decks are grouped by subject."

**1:05 - Quick quiz (go to Quick quiz, start it, answer two or three questions, show the results screen)**

"Next, the quick quiz. StudyMate turns any deck into a multiple choice quiz. The wrong answers come from other cards in the same deck, so you have to really know it. I get feedback as I go, and at the end I see my score and the exact ones I missed, so I know what to revise again."

**1:40 - Study timer (go to Study timer, start it, let it count a few seconds, point at the settings)**

"This is the study timer. It works like the Pomodoro method: study for 25 minutes, then take a 5 minute break. I can change both times. I pick the subject before I start, and when a study session finishes, the time is logged against that subject on its own. It also counts how many sessions I have finished today."

**2:05 - Subjects (go to Subjects, show the progress bars, add a subject or log time by hand)**

"Last, subjects. I set a target for each subject, like 3 hours a week for Biology. The progress bar shows how much of this week's target I have done, using the time from the timer. If I studied away from the timer, I can log the time by hand."

**2:25 - Closing (back to the Flashcards tab)**

"That is StudyMate. Flashcards, a quiz from your own cards, a timer that counts your study time, and weekly targets, all in one small app with no sign-up. Thank you for watching."

## After recording

- Upload to YouTube as unlisted (or public) and make sure embedding is allowed, or use Vimeo.
- Keep it under 3 minutes. If it runs long, trim the pauses between sections.
- Paste the video link into the Devpost submission.
